# Phase 8 — Carriers to ask and split-lane options

Date: 2026-09-14. Roadmap: phase 8, built after phase 7 (trip miles and truck
routes) is merged. Two additions to Lane Benchmark and the RFP lane panel.
**Carriers to ask** lists, by name only, carriers that know a lane when the
first search finds no carrier rate. **Split-lane options** shows, only on a
click, how a lane has been moved in legs by different carriers, with leg costs,
and estimates a split through a handoff point when the lane has no split
history. Direct shipments stay first and remain the default.

Build order and rollout: Carriers to ask is built first, then Split-lane
options. The work on branch `phase-8/carriers-and-split-lanes` starts from main
after phase 7 merges. Deploys go to PostProduc (alias `sandbox`) only;
production is not deployed.

## 2. Today (measured in PostProduc, 2026-09-14)

PostProduc is a copy of production whose data ends on 2026-08-14; the 712 RFP lanes in it belong to 5 test projects. Frequencies below are estimates.

- **First search**: city on both ends, 90-day lookback, accepted and declined carrier quotes plus bids (active bids only on Lane Benchmark; the RFP history request also counts expired bids), no mode filter; a rate is a row whose total is not 0 (`LaneSearchService` `countNonZero`). Nothing marks "no rate" today: Lane Benchmark implies it from the statistics, the RFP lane panel ignores them, Load history writes `No match` or `Done` with a blank median.
- **How often**: about 1 customer load in 3 had no priced carrier rate at city level in the 90 days before it was created (2,110 of 6,732): 26 % of direct loads, 77 % of split loads searched on their full lane. Counted by distinct lane it is 54 %, because most lanes have a single load.
- **Quotes**: `rtms__CarrierQuote__c` holds 404,381 quotes from 2018; the carrier is `rtms__Vendor__c` (indexed, equal to the displayed carrier on 2,000 of 2,000 recent quotes). The load lane keys (`rtms__City_Lane__c`, `rtms__State_Lane__c`, `rtms__Zip3_Lane__c`) are indexed External IDs; a destination-only pattern (`%:value`) is not selective.
- **Split shipments**: a Revenova freight plan links one customer load (`rtms__FreightPlanLoad__c.rtms__Type__c = 'Customer'`) to carrier legs (`'Carrier'`, ordered by `rtms__Number__c`). Customer loads leave `rtms__Freight_Plan__c` empty, so plans are found through the junction; junction rows carry 2026 creation dates (15,807 carrier rows in 2026 against 5,951 by their load's date, apparently re-created), so dates come from `rtms__Load__r.CreatedDate`. Only 276 of 3,992 split customer loads in a year have any carrier quote, while their 8,242 legs name 767 carriers. Of split loads whose full lane had no priced rate, 67 % had every leg priced at city level.
- **Yield of a deeper search** on the hardest no-rate lanes: older quotes (1 to 3 years) on the same lane name carriers on about 1 lane in 6 (31 of 180; 37 with no age limit), usually 1 or 2 names; the same state lane names carriers on about 9 lanes in 10 but with a median of 38 names, hence the top 10.
- **State codes**: Revenova reuses two-letter state codes across countries (NL is Newfoundland and Nuevo León, ME Maine and Estado de México, BC British Columbia and Baja California, CO Colorado and Coahuila); state lane `NL:QC` holds 880 Mexico to Canada loads and 10 Canadian ones. A stop account's `ShippingCountry` holds a country name (Canada, United States, Mexico; blank on about 4 % of recent loads), while `Pricing_Place__c.Country__c` holds a code (CA, US, MX).
- **Pollution**: in a year, intercompany vendors quoted 4,787 times (Tpml) and 1,918 times (Lessard org). The excluded-contact setting hides 4,433 of the Tpml quotes; the other 354 and every Lessard-org quote still show. Name rules would also catch carriers that look like third parties (RHINO EXPRESS S.A DE C.V - LESSARD, Transport Simon Lessard Inc - Qc; not verified). `Account.Black_List__c` is set on 548 carrier accounts (596 accepted quotes from 4 carriers in a year). Today's red results warning shows `Carrier_Att__c`, which joins two Transportation Profile flags: its blacklist text matches 0 quotes in a year, its "UNDER A CLAIM" text 2,897 quotes from 35 carriers.
- **Money**: every load is in CAD; a load's `rtms__Carrier_Quote_Total__c` holds CAD at the rate of the quote date (USD quotes × about 1.366). `Load_Cost_CAD__c` is unreliable for USD legs and `rtms__Vendor_Quote_Total__c` equals the carrier total. Split customer loads have no carrier total, so costs come from the legs. CAD is the corporate currency; USD is active with weekly dated exchange rates (`DatedConversionRate`: 369 USD rows, the latest starting 2026-08-11 at 0.7239 USD per CAD in PostProduc; the static `CurrencyType` rate is 0.7443). Whether production keeps the weekly rates current is to be confirmed.
- **Handoffs**: Laredo TX is the handoff on 1,677 of 1,771 Mexico to US or Canada splits; San Diego 72, Calexico 13. The border crossing fee is usually inside the Mexican leg's quote; yard, transload and cross-dock fees are almost never recorded.
- **Code budgets**: `LaneSearchRequest` holds 20 of PMD's 20 fields; after phase 7, class complexity is 37 of 40 in `LaneBenchmarkController` and in `RfpHistoryService`, and 39 in `RfpSecurityTest`; `RfpBulkService` is at 39 and `LaneSearchQueryBuilder` at 26; `rfpTool.js` is at 554 of its 560-line ceiling.

## 3. Carriers to ask

**Input**: the same request JSON the first search used (Lane Benchmark's service request; the lane panel's history request). The screen decides whether D2 holds from the search result it already has, then calls the server once, only when both ends have a place key.

**What the request decides** (both cards): the place keys, each end's precision and explicit postal prefix, and the mode, equipment and hazmat filters, applied to the load that matches the lane (a quote's load, a split's customer load, an estimate's leg load). Everything else is ignored: the lookback (each step has its own window), the currency (names come from any currency), the accepted/declined filter (both count; loads moved are accepted quotes), the source filter (quotes and split legs only, no bids), `excludeProjectId` and `includeExpiredBids`. Market Test quotes are left out, as in every lane search since phase 7 removed the Market Test flag (R110).

**Lane matching** (both cards): each end is matched the way the first search matched it: city, province or postal prefix; a radius end uses its place's city key. Quotes and split customer loads go through the same matcher (section 6).

**Stage 1, same lane** (last 3 years, non-cancelled loads):

- _Quotes_: carrier quotes on the lane, in two grouped counts by `rtms__Vendor__c` (SOQL has no conditional count): distinct loads quoted with the latest quote date, and distinct loads moved with `rtms__Accepted__c = true`. The carrier's name and `Black_List__c` come through `rtms__Vendor__r` in the same groups.
- _Split legs_: customer loads on the lane that belong to a freight plan, then that plan's carrier legs, grouped by the leg load's carrier (`rtms__Carrier__c`), counting distinct leg loads moved; the latest date is the customer load's date (R84).
- The two are merged per carrier (sources: Quote, Split leg) and ranked by loads moved, then loads quoted, then last seen. Every carrier found is shown.

**Stage 2, same state or province lane**, only when stage 1 has no carrier left after the removals below: over the last 365 days, quotes on loads whose `rtms__State_Lane__c` is the two places' `Province__c` codes, plus the carrier legs of freight plans whose customer load is on that state lane. Each end must also be in its place's country: the load's first stop account's `ShippingCountry` (origin) and last stop account's `ShippingCountry` (destination) must match the place's `Country__c`, with the code mapped to the country name the account holds; a load with a blank stop country does not count. Carriers are grouped and ranked by loads moved (accepted quotes and legs), then last seen, top 10 after the removals. Stage 2 is skipped when either place has a blank `Province__c`, or when both ends of the first search already used province keys (stage 1 covered that lane).

**Removals**: intercompany carriers from the D6 list are left out by the queries themselves, in every stage. Quotes from the excluded contact (`Contact_ID__c`, as in the first search) are not counted; the setting applies to quotes only, because split legs have no quote contact. Quotes whose customer name contains "Market Test" are not counted either, as in every lane search (R110). A stage counts as empty, and stage 2's top 10 is counted, after these removals.

**Each carrier shows**: name, loads moved, loads quoted, last seen, sources, a blacklisted badge when `Account.Black_List__c` is set, and a link to the account only when the user can read it.

**Names only**: Carriers to ask never selects or returns an amount. The result type has no amount field, and a test fails when one appears. Grouped counts stay capped at 50 groups. Freight plans and their legs are read as plain rows without amount fields, with a LIMIT, not grouped by plan, because an aggregate query returning more than 2,000 rows fails in Apex.

## 4. Split-lane options

**When**: on the click, with the request rules and lane matching of section 3. When either end of the first search has no place key, the card does not run and Lane Benchmark hides the **Split-lane options** button.

**History** (on click; last 12 months by the customer load's date, non-cancelled):

1. Find customer loads in freight plans on the lane, matched per end as in section 3 (city, province or postal prefix; a radius end uses its place's city key).
2. Read their plans' carrier legs in `rtms__Number__c` order, with each leg load's carrier (`rtms__Carrier__c`) and its `Black_List__c`, the leg's `rtms__City_Lane__c`, `rtms__Load_Status__c`, CAD `rtms__Carrier_Quote_Total__c` and its last stop's location account (`rtms__Last_Stop__r.rtms__Location2__r.Name`).
3. Leave out cancelled shipments (the customer load or any leg cancelled) and shipments with an intercompany carrier on any leg, because those leg costs are internal pass-throughs; in PostProduc that is about 42 shipments a year.
4. Group shipments by their handoff sequence, most shipments first; every group found is shown. A handoff reads "<city and province from the destination half of the leg's `rtms__City_Lane__c`> · <the leg's last stop account name>", for example "via Laredo, TX · SFMN Park ITC Laredo yard"; the stop's own `rtms__City__c` is blank on every leg.
5. For each group: shipments; for each leg position, its carriers (distinct, with legs moved, and a blacklisted badge when `Account.Black_List__c` is set) and the median of its priced leg totals; the **median total per shipment** over shipments whose legs are all priced, with that count. A leg position with no priced total shows names only.

**Currency**: the card has a **CAD | USD** switch. It starts on the RFP lane's currency in the lane panel, and on the currency filter on Lane Benchmark, or CAD without a filter. Every amount on the card is in the chosen currency: leg medians, totals per shipment, estimates and the handoff cost. Leg costs are stored in CAD; for USD, each load's CAD amount is multiplied by the USD `DatedConversionRate` whose period contains the load's `CreatedDate` (the latest rate for dates after the last period) before medians and sums are taken. Switching asks the server again; each currency's answer is cached.

**Estimates** (only when history finds no group):

1. Take the active handoff points from the D8 list; drop a point equal to the lane's origin or destination.
2. Keep points whose approximate distance origin → point → destination is at most 10 % above origin → destination (phase 7's `TripMilesService.approxMiles`: great-circle miles × the `Truck_Routing_Setting__mdt` Approx factor, 1.2, which cancels out of the ratio), at most 3, least extra distance first.
3. For each point, read two legs. Leg 1 is origin → point, with the origin end at the first search's precision for that end and the point end at city. Leg 2 is point → destination, with the point end at city and the destination end at the first search's precision for that end. Each leg read covers moved loads on that leg lane over the last 365 days, leaving out cancelled loads and loads whose carrier is on the D6 intercompany list, and takes carrier legs and direct loads with a positive CAD carrier total (`rtms__Carrier_Quote_Total__c`; a split customer load has no carrier total, so it never counts), the most recent 500 per leg. Each leg shows its carriers (with the blacklisted badge) and its median total.
4. Estimate = leg 1 median + leg 2 median + the typed handoff cost, labelled **Estimate**. A leg without priced loads has no median, and its option shows no estimate total.

**Direct stays in view**: next to each option the screen shows the median the first search already returned in the card's currency, with its code; nothing is shown when the first search has no median in that currency. The server is not asked for it; the lane panel keeps its first search's statistics (`statsByCurrency`) for this and for D2.

**Use as cost** (RFP lane panel, shown only on an option that has a total and only while the card shows the lane's own currency; an estimate needs a typed handoff cost, 0 allowed): fills the lane panel's price editor, as choosing a bid does. The cost becomes the option's total (history median total, or estimate including the typed handoff cost), the cost source becomes `Split` and any selected bid is cleared. The pricer saves with **Save price**, through the panel's existing `pricingchange` → `saveLanePricing` path. **Split** is not a selectable cost source button: it shows as the current source after Use as cost (or on a lane saved with it), and choosing another source replaces it. The margin then works as for any cost. When the pricer switches the card to the other currency, Use as cost is off with a short explanation.

## 5. Data and settings

- `RFP_Lane__c.Cost_Source__c` (restricted picklist) gains the value **Split**. Code that reads the source and changes: `rfpPricing` (`SOURCES` keeps its three selectable values; `costFor` treats Split as a stored cost and keeps the filled amount, the lane's `Cost__c` once saved), `rfpLanePanel` (its source options add Split only while the source is Split; the cost stays locked, as for any source but Manual), the `rfp-pricing` guide page's Cost source list (EN and FR) and the `rfpPricing` Jest tests. `rfpLaneFilter` (the Manual filter) and `RfpLaneController.saveLanePricing` (passes the source through) need no change. `RfpPricingMath` does not read the source, and bulk Apply median is unaffected (it keeps any cost whose source is not History median unless overwrite is chosen).
- The Excel export (`rfpWorkbook.js` `OUR_COLUMNS`) gains a **Cost source** column after Currency (Bid, History median, Manual or Split; blank when the lane has no cost), and the `rfp-filters-export` guide page (EN and FR) lists it.
- **Copy pricing** and the twin offer (`RfpBulkService.copyPricing`) treat a Split cost like any other stored cost: it is saved in its lane's currency, so no Split-specific rule is added (R100).
- **`Intercompany_Carrier__mdt`** (new, public): `Carrier_Account_Id__c` Text(18), `Note__c` Text(255); records for the two accounts in D6, `0011N00001JcHnvQAF` (Tpml) and `0011N00001JbQ7EQAV` (Lessard org).
- **`Handoff_Point__mdt`** (new, public): `Place_Key__c` Text(120) (a `Pricing_Place__c.Key__c`), `Active__c` Checkbox; records `laredo|tx|us`, `san diego|ca|us` and `calexico|ca|us`.
- No new object and no stored results: answers come from cacheable Apex. The new cards have no separate Refresh control. Each card shows **Retry** after a failure; Retry calls the server with a changing parameter so the browser cache is bypassed, and reopening a lane uses the cache.
- Both permission sets get class access to the two new controllers.

## 6. Apex behaviour

- `CarrierLeadController.findCarrierLeads(String requestJson, Integer attempt)` — `@AuraEnabled(cacheable=true)`; reads only the place keys, precisions, postal prefixes and the mode, equipment and hazmat filters from the request. It has no readable-project check, because no bid is read. `attempt` is 0 on the first call and grows with each Retry; the server ignores it.
- `CarrierLeadService` — reads quotes, freight plans and legs in system mode, like `LaneSearchService`, because carrier quotes inherit Private load sharing; one user-mode `Account` read decides each link.
- `CarrierLeadResult` — `leads` (carrier Id, name, loads moved, loads quoted, last seen, sources, blacklisted, can open), `stage` (`Same lane`, `State lane`, `None`), `truncated`; no `Decimal` field anywhere.
- `SplitLaneController.splitOptions(String requestJson, String currencyCode, Integer attempt)` — `@AuraEnabled(cacheable=true)`; `currencyCode` is `CAD` or `USD` (anything else is refused); `SplitLaneService` reads freight plans and loads in system mode and returns `SplitLaneResult` (the currency, the start date of the latest exchange rate used when USD, history groups or estimate options, each leg's carriers with legs moved and blacklisted, medians and counts). It does not re-run the lane search and carries no direct median. The handoff cost is added on the screen. `attempt` works as for Carriers to ask.
- `LaneKeyMatcher` (new, extracted from `LaneSearchQueryBuilder`) builds each end's lane-key WHERE fragment and binds for a precision and a field path prefix (`rtms__Load__r.` on quotes and freight-plan junction rows, none on loads). The lane search, `CarrierLeadService` and `SplitLaneService` use it; the search's conditions and results do not change.
- `IntercompanyCarriers` and `HandoffPoints` read the two custom metadata lists, with test overrides (custom metadata cannot be inserted in tests), like `LaneSearchExclusions`.
- `ExchangeRates` (new) reads the USD `DatedConversionRate` periods covering the read window in one query and converts a CAD amount for a date (the latest rate after the last period); `SplitLaneService` uses it only when the card's currency is USD. Tests use a rate override, because dated rates cannot be inserted in tests.
- The results warning text is built in `LaneSearchService` instead of read from `Carrier_Att__c`: " THE CARRIER IS BLACK-LISTED " when the quote vendor's `rtms__Vendor__r.Black_List__c` is set, then the existing " UNDER A CLAIM " text when the Transportation Profile's `Outstanding_Claim__c` is set; `LaneSearchQueryBuilder`'s select reads both flags. Bid rows whose `Benchmark_Rate__c.Carrier__c` account is blacklisted get the blacklist text. The warning shows in Lane Benchmark results and in the RFP lane panel's history results, which use the same search and component.
- New classes carry the logic; `LaneSearchService`, `LaneSearchRequest`, `LaneBenchmarkController`, `RfpHistoryService` and `RfpBulkService` keep their budgets, and `LaneSearchRequest` gains no field.

## 8. Limits, errors, security

- Carriers to ask: about 5 queries for stage 1 (places, two quote counts, plans with their legs, the user-mode Account read) and 3 more for stage 2 (two quote counts, plans with their legs); this is an estimate, to be measured in the browser check. Each grouped count is capped at 50 carriers and each plan read has a LIMIT; never more than 3 years, never a radius over 3 years; a query-row check stops early and shows "The list may be incomplete".
- Split history: one query on `rtms__FreightPlan__c` whose `Id` is in a semi-join on customer junction rows (`rtms__Type__c = 'Customer'`, lane keys, `CreatedDate` in the last 365 days and status not `Cancelled` on `rtms__Load__r`), with the child subquery `rtms__Freight_Plan_Loads__r` returning the carrier legs and their load fields ordered by `rtms__Number__c`; there is no separate leg read (SOQL refuses a semi-join from `rtms__FreightPlanLoad__c` to itself and nested semi-joins); estimates: at most 3 points × 2 leg reads, each the most recent 500 moved loads; in USD, one more query reads the dated exchange rates.
- Each card fails on its own with a short message and **Retry**; the first search's rates and rows are never held back. Cacheable answers make reopening a lane instant; Retry passes a new `attempt` so it reaches the server.
- Carrier names and carrier costs are already visible to everyone who runs a lane search. Carriers to ask shows no amount at all; split options show leg costs the way Lane Benchmark results already show quote totals, and never name the customer.
- Use as cost uses the lane panel's existing user-mode save; the new picklist value needs no new permission.

## 10. Rulings

- R80 — "no rate" is judged in the pricing currency (D2), matching how an RFP lane's median works.
- R81 — Carriers to ask stops at the first stage that has a carrier left after the intercompany and excluded-contact removals; stage 2 never mixes with stage 1.
- R82 — "loads moved" counts accepted quotes and split legs; stage 1 ranks by loads moved, then loads quoted, then last seen; stage 2 ranks by loads moved, then last seen.
- R83 — a radius first search uses the two places' city keys for the deeper steps; no radius search spans 3 years.
- R84 — split history dates come from the customer load, because freight-plan junction rows carry 2026 creation dates.
- R85 — the split total is the median of each shipment's own leg sum, over shipments with every leg priced, not a sum of leg medians.
- R86 — estimates read Revenova load totals in CAD on the two leg lanes over 365 days, not quote currency buckets, so they stay in CAD like history (owner, 2026-09-14 21:52: load costs in CAD, chosen over normal leg searches and over converted searches, because most Mexican legs are quoted in USD and normal searches often have no CAD median).
- R87 — a shipment with a cancelled leg or an intercompany carrier on a leg is left out of split history (owner, 2026-09-14 21:52).
- R88 — Use as cost works while the split card shows the lane's own currency; the card converts only its own amounts (owner, 2026-09-14 22:17).
- R89 — intercompany carriers are listed by account Id; the existing excluded-contact setting stays as it is and applies to quotes only.
- R90 — blacklisted carriers stay visible with a badge, and the existing results warning takes its blacklist text from the same account flag, on quote and bid rows, while its claim text stays.
- R91 — nothing is stored: cacheable Apex serves repeated views; there is no Refresh control, and a card's Retry after a failure passes a changing parameter so it reaches the server.
- R92 — new classes hold the logic, so the existing search classes keep their PMD budgets and field caps.
