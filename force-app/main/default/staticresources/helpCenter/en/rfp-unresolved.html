What it means

A lane shows Unresolved when its origin or destination city (or postal prefix) is not in the place dictionary, often because of a typo, an unusual spelling or a very small town. The lane is imported but cannot be searched.

Fixing it

In the lane row, the unresolved end shows an Origin or Destination field with a Precision picker. Type the place and pick it from the list (or search the map). The end is saved at once and, when both ends are resolved, the lane's history is loaded automatically. Use Retry failed only for lanes that show Failed.

Prevention

Ask your admin to add an alias for the spelling the customer uses, so the next file resolves on its own.
